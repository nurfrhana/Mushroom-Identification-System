# User Stories

1. As a user, I want the system to recognise the type of mushroom correctly, so that there will be no misidentification of mushroom. (FR1)
    Acceptance criteria:
    - The photos taken/uploaded must shows clear view of the mushroom.
    - The photos must include the muhsroom cap and under cap.
2. As an administrator, I want the system to provide detailed explanation of the mushroom, so that the user immediately aware of the edibility of the muhsroom on the spot. (FR2)
    Acceptance criteria:
    - The system will provide the mushroom species name, common called name (if any), characteristics, habitat, and season information.
    - The system will also provide detailed explanation of the mushroom toxic look alike (if any) within the same page to avoid misidentification.
3. As an app developer, I want the app to have a foraging diary of users finding, so that they can see the map of their finds. (FR3)
    Acceptance criteria:
    - User must open their GPS location, to save the location of their finds.
    - User must fill in the date and upload the photos of their finds to save in the diary.
