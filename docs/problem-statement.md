# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Foragers and people that lives in rural area that forage wild mushrooms.

## What exactly is going wrong?
They collect wild mushrooms without deep understanding about their edibility and had been relying on passed down information from their ancestor or family causing them to misidentify few muhsroom.

## Why does it matter?
These issue caused people to have mushroom poisoning because of the recurring try-and-error method and it will keep happening if there is no proper solution taken.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Foragers and rural people need a mushroom identification system that gives user instant confirmation of a mushroom edibility. This is because rural residents mostly had been relying on passed down information and trial-and-error method. This issue can caused people to high possibility of mushroom poisoning case as the method used to identify mushroom edibilty is unreliable and risky.

## Evidence
Cases of mushroom poisoning have been recorded in Malaysia up until 2026, which is the most recent case occurred in Sik, Kedah, on June 29 where a couple from Padang Besar were hospitalised after suffering severe food-poisoning symptoms believed to be caused by eating wild mushrooms picked near their house. It is stated that they consume the mushrooms after mistakenly believing that they were edible (Couple hospitalised after wild mushroom meal in Padang Besar. 29 June 2026. malaymail. https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/06/29/couple-hospitalised-after-wild-mushroom-meal-in-padang-besar/225694).
